Who I Am

About Me

I'm Sakshith C Shetty — a BCA graduate from Mangalore University turned full-time offensive security practitioner. I operate as SHADOW-269 on Bugcrowd, hunting real vulnerabilities in production environments.

My focus is the intersection of web application security, API attack surfaces, and mobile app analysis. I've spent the last two years moving from academic understanding to live engagements: bypassing WAFs, chaining access control flaws, and producing findings that actually help security teams remediate — not just checkbox reports.

When I'm not finding vulnerabilities, I'm building tools, studying the latest CVEs, and contributing to the security community. I believe offensive security done right is a fundamentally creative discipline — every application has a unique attack surface, and the best findings always come from genuine curiosity.

Mission "Make systems harder to break by being the one who breaks them first — with clarity, responsibility, and a complete remediation path."
